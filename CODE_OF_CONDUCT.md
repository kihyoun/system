# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome participation regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion or
sexual identity and orientation.

Treat people with respect. Give constructive feedback, consider different
perspectives, accept responsibility for mistakes and focus criticism on the
work. Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, doxxing and publishing private information without consent
are unacceptable.

This policy covers project repositories, reviews, issue discussions and other
project spaces, including public representation of the project. Maintainers may
edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report privately to [kihyoun@gmail.com](mailto:kihyoun@gmail.com), including when
you do not have GitLab source-project access. Include enough context for review
and avoid unnecessary personal data. Maintainers track reports in confidential
GitLab issues; access is limited to the reporter where applicable, authorized
project members and instance administrators. Existing source-project members may
use the [confidential report form](https://gitlab.system-bootstrapper.com/system-bootstrapper/system/-/issues/new?issuable_template=Confidential&issue[confidential]=true)
and confirm **Confidential** before submitting. The preset does not enforce the
setting if you clear it.

Maintainers review reports privately, seek context from the people involved
when appropriate, and protect reporter privacy as far as investigation allows.
A maintainer involved in a report should recuse themselves; request another
maintainer in the private thread. Responses depend on severity and repetition:
clarification or correction, a warning with expectations, a temporary restriction,
or permanent exclusion. Maintainers explain participation restrictions privately
when possible. Ask for reconsideration in the same confidential thread, including
new context. No fixed response or appeal deadline is promised.

These principles draw on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
